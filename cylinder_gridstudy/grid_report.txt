  The L2->L3->L4 sequence is not monotone -- the mean flow still
  carries a startup-seeded asymmetry that each mesh damps at its own
  rate, so differences in the mean mix discretization error with that
  residue. It clears by L5. Any order quoted above is therefore an
  upper bound read off the two finest levels, not a fitted rate; the
  symmetry error below is the cleaner statement and needs no reference
  solution at all.
